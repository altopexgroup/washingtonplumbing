Privacy Policy

Plain-language privacy policy for our call-only plumbing website serving Seattle, Washington and surrounding King County communities.

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Washington Plumbing operates the website located at seattle.washingtonplumbing.best. Because our service relies exclusively on direct phone communication rather than web forms, user accounts, or newsletter sign-ups, we collect minimal information when you browse our pages.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, standard web server logs and basic analytics tools may collect non-personal technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device operating system, referring pages, and the specific pages viewed during your visit. This information is used strictly to maintain website performance, analyze traffic trends, and improve user navigation.

Phone Calls and Communications

When you initiate contact by calling our phone number at (253) 201-3775, your telephone number and details discussed during the conversation are used solely to address your plumbing inquiry and coordinate service dispatch. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your phone number or personal details to third-party marketing entities.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website utilizes basic functional cookies and analytics to ensure optimal loading speeds and mobile responsiveness. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies if you prefer, though certain navigation features may be affected.

Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this privacy policy to reflect changes in technical operations or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please call us directly at (253) 201-3775.

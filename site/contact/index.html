Quick Safety Steps for Leaks and Backups

If you are currently experiencing an active pipe leak or severe wastewater backup while preparing to call our office, taking immediate safety precautions can prevent extensive water damage. Locate your primary water shutoff valve (usually situated in the basement, utility room, or crawlspace near the front foundation wall) and turn it clockwise until fully closed. If water is pooling near electrical outlets or appliances, turn off power at your main breaker panel if safe to do so.
